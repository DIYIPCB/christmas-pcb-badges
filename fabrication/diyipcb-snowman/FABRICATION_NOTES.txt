PCB FABRICATION NOTES - diyipcb-snowman - Revision R1 - 2026-11-25
Decorative passive badge. Fabricate as TWO SIDED PCB, no electrical assembly.
Base material: FR-4; nominal finished thickness 1.6 mm; copper: 1 oz each side.
Surface finish: OSP on all exposed copper. Do not substitute HASL or ENIG.
Solder mask: Red, both sides. Legend: WHITE ONLY, both sides.
Profile envelope (centerline): 38.376 x 64.842 mm.
Use supplied Edge.Cuts Gerber for actual routed profile; NOT a rectangular board.
Routing tool: 1.0 mm diameter or smaller for concave details; deburr edges.
One NPTH hanging hole, finished diameter 3.2 mm. Empty PTH file is intentional.
No electrical connection between top/bottom copper is needed. No plated drill holes.
F.Mask and B.Mask Gerbers define OPENINGS. Copper artwork is decorative.
No solder paste, stencil, components, BOM or pick-and-place file required.
All layers are provided in standard top-view PCB coordinates. Do NOT mirror files.
Do not add supplier order IDs or remove graphic copper islands / white legend fills.
Snowman: large WHITE SILKSCREEN FILLS over RED soldermask are intentional.
Red face details and brand on snowman are gaps in white ink, revealing red mask.
There is NO red legend ink, no white soldermask, no UV/color printing.
Keep full supplied legend polygons. Confirm white coverage and negative detail capability
in CAM before production. Do not run an automatic 'remove large silkscreen' cleanup.
Copper-to-edge target >=0.50 mm; legend-to-mask-opening >=0.20 mm.
Keep OSP surface protected in individual packaging; copper color is illustrative.
Single design per ZIP; manufacture quantity and any panelization separately as ordered.
Manufacturing tolerances: standard factory process, subject to CAM confirmation.
